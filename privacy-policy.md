# Privacy Policy for Hewa App

**Effective date:** 20 November 2026

## English

Hewa is a free weather app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where that information goes, and the choices you have.

### Summary

- Hewa has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
- Your location, saved places and settings are stored only on your phone.
- To show the weather, Hewa sends map coordinates or the name of a place you search for to the free weather services listed below. It does not send your name, phone number, email or contacts.
- We do not sell, rent or trade any information.

### Information the app uses

**Location (only if you allow it).** When you grant location permission, Hewa reads your phone's location while the app is open. It uses it to show the weather where you are. Hewa does not track your location over time. If you refuse permission, the app shows the weather for Dar es Salaam instead.

**Background updates.** If your phone allows background refresh, Hewa updates the weather about every two hours for the last location it saved. This keeps the widget, the morning briefing and severe-weather alerts up to date. Hewa does not read your location in the background.

**Places you search for and save.** The text you type in the search box is sent to the geocoding service to find matching places. Places you save are stored only on your phone.

**Settings.** Your units, language, notification choices and the farmer and sea modes are stored only on your phone.

**Weather cache.** The latest weather for each place is stored on your phone, so the app still works when you are offline.

**Notifications.** The morning briefing and severe-weather alerts are created on your phone. Hewa does not use push servers or send your information anywhere to deliver them. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

**Voice readout.** When you tap Listen, the weather is read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Hewa does not record audio or use the microphone.

**Sharing.** When you tap Share, Hewa creates the weather image on your phone. It is sent only to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp.

**Insight reminders.** If you turn them on, reminders such as lightning or water-safety tips are planned on your phone from the forecast. Nothing extra is sent anywhere.

**Trips.** If you turn on commute alerts, your morning and evening trip times are stored only on your phone. They are used to schedule reminders on your phone.

**Data use counter.** Hewa counts how much data its weather downloads use each day. The number is stored only on your phone.

**Feedback email.** If you choose to email us from the Settings screen, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app.

### Services that receive information

To show weather information, the app connects directly from your phone to the services below. Each one receives your device's IP address and the request itself. They are independent companies with their own privacy policies.

| Service | What it receives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) | Coordinates of the place shown, and your search text | Forecast, air quality, sea conditions, past rainfall, river flow, place search |
| [BigDataCloud](https://www.bigdatacloud.com/privacy) | Your coordinates (only if your phone cannot name the place itself) | Turning coordinates into a place name |
| Your phone's built-in geocoder (Google on Android, Apple on iOS) | Your coordinates | Turning coordinates into a place name |
| [OpenWeatherMap](https://openweather.co.uk/privacy-policy) | Nothing about you except your IP address | Weather icon images |
| [Expo](https://expo.dev/privacy) (EAS Update) | App version and basic device information | Checking for and delivering app updates |

All connections use encrypted HTTPS.

### How long information is kept

Everything Hewa stores is kept on your phone until you remove it. You can delete saved places in Settings. To delete everything, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. We do not keep copies of this information. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to respond.

### Your choices

- Allow or deny location permission at any time in your phone's settings.
- Turn notifications on or off in Hewa's Settings or your phone's settings.
- Remove the widget from your home screen whenever you like.
- Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove everything stored on your phone.

### Children

Hewa is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information in a new way, we will update this policy and change the effective date above.

## Swahili

Hewa ni programu ya bure ya hali ya hewa. Sera hii inaeleza taarifa ambazo programu inatumia, zinakokwenda, na machaguo uliyonayo.

### Kwa ufupi

- Hewa haina akaunti, haina matangazo, na haitumii zana za kufuatilia au takwimu za matumizi.
- Mahali ulipo, maeneo uliyohifadhi na mipangilio yako huhifadhiwa kwenye simu yako pekee.
- Ili kuonyesha hali ya hewa, Hewa hutuma kwa huduma za hali ya hewa zilizoorodheshwa hapa chini viwianishi vya mahali, au jina la mahali unalotafuta. Haitumi jina lako, namba ya simu, barua pepe wala anwani zako.
- Hatuuzi, hatukodishi wala hatubadilishani taarifa yoyote.

### Taarifa ambazo programu inatumia

**Mahali ulipo (ukiruhusu tu).** Ukitoa ruhusa ya mahali, Hewa husoma mahali simu yako ilipo wakati programu iko wazi. Huyatumia kuonyesha hali ya hewa ya mahali ulipo. Hewa haifuatilii mienendo yako. Usiporuhusu, programu huonyesha hali ya hewa ya Dar es Salaam.

**Masasisho ya chinichini.** Simu yako ikiruhusu, Hewa husasisha hali ya hewa takriban kila saa mbili kwa mahali ilipohifadhi mara ya mwisho. Hii huweka wijeti, taarifa ya asubuhi na tahadhari za hali mbaya ya hewa kuwa za sasa. Hewa haisomi mahali ulipo ikiwa chinichini.

**Maeneo unayotafuta na kuhifadhi.** Maandishi unayoandika kwenye kisanduku cha kutafuta hutumwa kwa huduma ya kutafuta maeneo. Maeneo unayohifadhi hubaki kwenye simu yako pekee.

**Mipangilio.** Vipimo, lugha, machaguo ya arifa, na hali ya mkulima na ya bahari huhifadhiwa kwenye simu yako pekee.

**Hifadhi ya muda ya hali ya hewa.** Hali ya hewa ya karibuni kwa kila mahali huhifadhiwa kwenye simu yako, ili programu ifanye kazi hata bila mtandao.

**Arifa.** Taarifa ya asubuhi na tahadhari za hali mbaya ya hewa hutengenezwa kwenye simu yako. Hewa haitumii seva za arifa wala kutuma taarifa zako popote ili kuziwasilisha. Unaweza kuzizima kwenye Mipangilio ya programu au ya simu yako.

**Kusoma kwa sauti.** Ukibonyeza Sikiliza, hali ya hewa husomwa kwa sauti na injini ya sauti ya simu yako. Hewa hairekodi sauti wala kutumia kipaza sauti.

**Kushiriki.** Ukibonyeza Shiriki, Hewa hutengeneza picha ya hali ya hewa kwenye simu yako. Hutumwa tu kwa programu unayochagua, kama WhatsApp.

**Vikumbusho vya maarifa.** Ukiviwasha, vikumbusho kama vya radi au usalama wa maji hupangwa kwenye simu yako kutokana na utabiri. Hakuna taarifa za ziada zinazotumwa popote.

**Safari.** Ukiwasha tahadhari za safari, muda wa safari zako za asubuhi na jioni huhifadhiwa kwenye simu yako pekee. Hutumika kupanga vikumbusho kwenye simu yako.

**Kipimo cha data.** Hewa huhesabu kiasi cha data kinachotumiwa kila siku na upakuaji wa hali ya hewa. Hesabu hiyo huhifadhiwa kwenye simu yako pekee.

**Barua pepe ya maoni.** Ukichagua kututumia barua pepe kutoka skrini ya Mipangilio, tunapokea anwani yako ya barua pepe na ulichoandika. Tunavitumia kukujibu na kuboresha programu tu.

| [BigDataCloud](https://www.bigdatacloud.com/privacy) | Viwianishi vyako (ikiwa tu simu yako haiwezi kutaja jina la mahali yenyewe) | Kubadilisha viwianishi kuwa jina la mahali |
| Huduma ya ndani ya simu yako (Google kwenye Android, Apple kwenye iOS) | Viwianishi vyako | Kubadilisha viwianishi kuwa jina la mahali |

Miunganisho yote hutumia HTTPS iliyosimbwa.
